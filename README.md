# PrepTalk AI

## 🚀 AI-Powered Mock Interview & Interview Preparation Platform

PrepTalk AI is an intelligent AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers improve interview performance through AI-driven mock interviews, speech analysis, and performance evaluation.

The platform simulates realistic interview environments, evaluates candidate responses, analyzes communication quality, and provides personalized feedback for improvement.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Many candidates struggle with:

- Lack of realistic interview practice
- Nervousness and low confidence during interviews
- Limited personalized feedback
- Poor preparation for job-specific interview rounds

PrepTalk AI addresses these challenges using AI-driven interview simulations and smart performance analytics.

---

## 💡 Solution

PrepTalk AI creates an interactive AI interview ecosystem that:

1. Generates interview questions based on selected roles and experience levels
2. Conducts AI-powered mock interviews
3. Accepts voice and text-based responses
4. Evaluates candidate answers using AI
5. Analyzes communication and response quality
6. Provides personalized feedback and improvement suggestions
7. Generates detailed interview scorecards and performance analytics

---

## 🌟 Features

### 🎯 Interview Simulation
- AI-generated mock interviews
- Role-based interview questions
- Experience-level customization
- Voice and text response support
- Configurable number of interview questions

### 📊 Performance Evaluation
- AI-powered answer scoring
- Candidate answer vs expected answer comparison
- Performance analytics dashboard
- AI-generated feedback
- Hire / Maybe / No Hire recommendation

### 🧠 Behavioral & Communication Analysis
- Speech and communication analysis
- Confidence and hesitation monitoring
- Attention tracking
- Interview performance insights

### 🔒 Interview Monitoring
- Tab-switch detection
- Real-time interview environment monitoring

---

## 🔄 Workflow

1. Candidate selects:
   - Target role
   - Experience level
   - Number of interview questions

2. AI generates role-specific interview questions.

3. Candidate answers using:
   - Voice input
   - Text input

4. AI evaluates responses in real-time.

5. System analyzes:
   - Communication quality
   - Confidence level
   - Behavioral signals

6. Final dashboard displays:
   - Overall score
   - Hire recommendation
   - Expected vs candidate answer comparison
   - Skipped questions
   - Performance charts
