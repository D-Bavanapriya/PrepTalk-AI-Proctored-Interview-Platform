# PrepTalk-AI-Proctored-Interview-Platform

## 🚀 AI-Powered Mock Interview & Candidate Assessment System

PrepTalk is an intelligent AI-powered interview preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers improve interview performance through real-time AI interaction, speech analysis, and behavioral monitoring.

The platform simulates realistic mock interviews, evaluates candidate responses, analyzes confidence levels, and provides personalized feedback for improvement.

---

## 🌟 Features

-  AI-Based Mock Interviews
-  Resume & Job Description Analysis
-  Dynamic Interview Question Generation
-  Emotion & Confidence Detection
-  Speech & Communication Analysis
-  Performance Analytics Dashboard
-  Real-Time AI Feedback
-  Proctored Interview Environment

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement

Many candidates struggle with:

- Lack of realistic interview practice
  
- Nervousness and low confidence
  
- Limited personalized feedback
  
- Poor preparation for job-specific interviews

PrepTalk addresses these challenges using AI-driven interview simulations and smart performance analytics.

---

## 💡 Solution

PrepTalk creates an interactive AI interview ecosystem that:

1. Generates questions from resumes and job descriptions
   
2. Conducts mock interviews
 
3. Analyzes facial expressions and communication
  
4. Detects hesitation and confidence levels
   
5. Provides personalized feedback and improvement suggestions
